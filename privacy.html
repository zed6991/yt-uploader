Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

yt-uploader is a personal tool that uploads videos to YouTube channels owned by its operator. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

When you sign in, yt-uploader asks for permission to manage your YouTube account and upload videos (the youtube and youtube.upload scopes). It uses this only to read the name of your channel and to upload videos and their titles, descriptions and settings that you choose.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is saved only on the computer running yt-uploader. It is not sent to any server operated by yt-uploader, and there is no such server.

Sharing

yt-uploader does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Data goes only between your computer and Google's YouTube API.

Google API Services User Data Policy

yt-uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke yt-uploader's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the saved token from the computer running the tool.
